The destination wedding timeline: a 12-month blueprint

When to scout, when to book the block, when to fly the florist in.

By the Wedlock celebration desk · 28 January 2026

A destination wedding is two projects at once: a wedding, and moving a few hundred people to another city. Start the second one early and the first one becomes calm.

12 to 10 months out: choose the place

Fix a budget range and a rough guest count before you look at a single venue. Then shortlist two or three destinations and visit them together — the right venue is usually obvious within an hour of walking it.

9 to 6 months out: book the block

Block rooms close to the venue as soon as the date is fixed, and send save-the-dates so guests can book flights early. This is also when décor, catering and artists are contracted.

Guests remember how easy it was to arrive long after they forget the centrepieces.

The last month: rehearse everything

Share a final itinerary with every guest, confirm every pickup, and walk the venue with your planner the day before each event. The wedding week should hold no new decisions.
