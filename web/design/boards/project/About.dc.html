Our story

Born in Patna, built on the lineage of Eventoss.

At Wedlock, we deeply appreciate the importance of this special journey. We began in Patna in 2014, carrying the production craft of Eventoss — the region’s leading marketing and events agency — into weddings. A run of Patna weddings built our name for comfort matched with grandeur.

Today we are planners, stylists, carpenters, light designers, choreographers, travel agents and — when needed — crisis negotiators, with teams in Patna, Ranchi and New Delhi and a destination desk that has scouted 40+ cities in 14 countries.
